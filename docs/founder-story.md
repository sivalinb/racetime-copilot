## Why I’m building RaceTime Copilot

I’m Siva Babu—an ultrarunner, race organizer and crew member. I follow ultra athletes closely because I know how much goes into reaching the start line, staying out on the course and making it to the finish.

The races I follow can be livestreamed for more than 24 hours. I want to follow the whole story, but I also need to sleep and work. When I step away, part of my mind stays with the race: *What happened while I was away? How are the athletes I’m following doing? Which moments did I miss?*

Coming back often means scrubbing through hours of footage to piece the story together. That is the problem that led me to build RaceTime Copilot.

I want to stay connected to the sport and the people I care about, while still being present for the rest of my life.

## What RaceTime does

RaceTime lets you choose an available race-video interval and ask a question about it. For example: **“What happened between 06:00 and 06:15?”** Set those same start and end times in the app; they control the interval.

You add a YouTube link or video, select the time range and ask what you want to know. RaceTime retrieves existing observations or inspects the footage when needed, then produces a recap with timestamped evidence you can review. It applies your time and spoiler boundaries and shows gaps or uncertainty when the evidence is incomplete.

It is being built for fans catching up, crews following athletes, and organizers reviewing race moments. My aim is simple: **spend less time searching through the broadcast and more time understanding the race.**

*Current scope: questions depend on footage the app can access and process. Live questions use captured coverage; a pasted link does not automatically record an entire 24-hour broadcast. See the [product status](product-status.md) for tested workflows and remaining limitations.*
